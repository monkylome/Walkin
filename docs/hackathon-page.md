About the hackathon
A one-day hackathon in Athens. Apply as a team of two. Build for 12 hours on May 28. Finalists present on stage at Panathēnea on May 29 — one of Europe's leading gatherings for founders and builders — in front of investors and a large audience.

Speakers this year include founders and executives from OpenAI, Nvidia, Netlify, Deel, Runway, and Tools for Humanity.

Florent is a Nordic fund. We host one hackathon a year in Athens — one of Europe's most energetic cities for builders.

Theme
AI for the Real World
Not demos. Not research. Build AI that solves real problems for real people — in how they work, learn, and make decisions.

AI for complex work

Real-world workflows are fragmented and manual. Build AI that fixes that.
