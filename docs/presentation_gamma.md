# WalkIn
**The AI salesman that makes you move.**
Team: George Tzimokas & Arsenis Tsn

---

# The Problem

Everyday life creates urgent buying needs — and existing tools fail to solve them.

- Google Maps → shows stores, not products
- E-commerce → shows products, not local availability
- Yelp / Foursquare → venues, not stock

**Result:** Wasted trips, lost time, and local stores losing customers to online platforms.

---

# The Solution

WalkIn is a mobile app (iOS & Android) that answers one question:

> "Where can I find this product near me, right now?"

Not browsing. Not e-commerce. A tool for immediate local purchasing decisions.

**Why now:** "Near me" searches are surging. Local retail needs digital visibility. AI for natural language search is finally ready.

---

# Hero Use Case

> "George needs an Allen key M6 for a repair today. He opens WalkIn, types his request, and in 3 seconds sees it's available at a hardware store 400m away."

> "Nick has run a small hardware store for 15 years. No e-shop, no SEO. WalkIn makes him visible exactly when someone nearby needs what he has."

---

# How It Works

1. User searches in natural language (text or voice)
2. WalkIn returns nearby results with real-time availability
3. User sees: distance, travel time, availability status
4. Decision: visit the store or reserve the product for 2 hours

**Key features:** proximity filters · list + map view · product cards · Google Maps navigation · similar product suggestions

> *Google Maps knows every store. WalkIn knows what's inside them.*

---

# The AI Layer

The AI acts as a **smart local salesperson** — visible to the user, not just running in the background.

- Understands free-form descriptions ("that yellow desk lamp with the wooden base")
- Suggests similar alternatives when no exact match exists
- Explains why a result is relevant

**Fine-tuned LLM trained on the language of retail.** Understands how people actually talk about the things they need.

Every store earns a **Reliability Score**. High scorers display a **"Verified Stock"** badge — building user trust and incentivizing stores to keep data accurate.

---

# Business Model

**Free for users.** Stores subscribe:

- **Starter** — Basic visibility in a limited radius
- **Dreamer** — Increased visibility + **Demand Intelligence**: see what users nearby search for but can't find
- **Wander** — Priority placement + full Demand Intelligence analytics

**Launch incentive:** First 50 stores join free — no time limit, no strings attached. A bet on the product.

> Demand Intelligence turns WalkIn into a **business intelligence platform**: merchants learn what to stock based on real local demand.

---

# Market & Go-to-Market

**Greece:** ~200,000 active local stores · Athens & Thessaloniki pilot · low competition

**EU:** ~6 million local stores with no real-time product visibility online

**Go-to-Market:** Partnership with **ESCE (National Confederation of Greek Commerce)** to access the local merchant network. Their endorsement removes onboarding friction. We onboard 20–30 stores per neighborhood, then open to users — and repeat.
