# Walkin — Hackathon Presentation Script

**Format:** 2-minute video
**Tone:** Fast, visual, conversational

---

## 1. Hook + Problem (0:00 – 0:15)

> "You need a paracetamol. Or a phone charger. Or a specific drill bit. You know a store nearby probably has it — but which one? Is it in stock? Is it on your way?"

- You either waste time calling around, walking to the wrong store, or just order online and wait 2 days for something that's 300 meters away.
- Local stores have what you need — there's just no way to find it.

---

## 2. Walkin — The Solution (0:15 – 0:30)

> "Walkin connects you to real-time local inventory. Search, find, reserve — all before you leave."

- Real-time stock & pricing from stores around you
- Sort by distance or price
- Pay & Reserve items so they're waiting when you arrive

---

## 3. Why Both Sides Win (0:30 – 0:45)

**For customers:**
- No €3–5 delivery fees for something that's around the corner
- No 2-day waits — get it in minutes
- No walking into 3 stores hoping one has what you need in stock

**For stores:**
- Lower fees than other platforms — customers come to you
- Visibility to people who never knew they existed
- More reviews, more repeat visits, zero ad spend

---

## 4. Demo (0:45 – 1:35)

### Flow A — "I need something now" (25s)
1. Open app → tap AI button
2. Type: "I need Depon right now"
3. App detects location → shows map with price pins around you
4. AI replies: "MediCare Plus has Depon for €3.20, 4 min away"
5. Tap Reserve → done. It's held for you.

### Flow B — "On my way somewhere" (25s)
1. Type: "Going to Piraeus, I need a phone charger on the way"
2. AI understands the route → shows directions with the store as a stop
3. Map draws the actual driving route: You → Store → Piraeus
4. Tap Directions → opens navigation with the stop included

---

## 5. Future (1:35 – 1:50)

- **Live inventory sync** — POS integrations so stock updates automatically
- **AI Platforms Integration** — MCP Integration with Chat GPT, Gemini, Claude, even Google Maps
- **Expansion** — every neighborhood store becomes discoverable: pharmacies, hardware, electronics, convenience

---

## 6. Close (1:50 – 2:00)

> "Stop ordering online what's already around the corner."

**Walkin — find it nearby, get it now.**
